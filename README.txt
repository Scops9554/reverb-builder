A program that you can use to build feedback delay network reverberators.

Its components are a delay, a bank of parallel delay lines, Householder and Hadamard matrices, highpass, lowpass and biquad filters. This means that it is simple but flexible enough to produce most sorts of FDN reverberators.

CAUTION: if you use a biquad filter to boost certain frequencies, and they are not compensated to unity gain or less by other components in the program, the system will very quickly build up energy and you will blow your ears out. You have been warned.
